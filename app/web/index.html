📡 WebSocket RTT
WebSocket RTT. Measured live — the profiler opens a real RFC-6455 WebSocket to wss://ws-subscriptions-clob.polymarket.com/ws/market, sends protocol PING frames, and times the PONG round-trip across multiple samples. The card’s native title shows the run’s sample count and the exact URL. This is the channel Polymarket uses for live orderbook updates, so its RTT dominates copy-trade freshness more than HTTP REST does. Note: measured server-side (not from your browser).

Order Book VWAP Slippage Matrix

Evaluated across live Polymarket 5m & 15m binary outcome orderbooks
How to read this matrix: each row is the size of your copy-trade (top of your $100 bankroll). The two % columns are VWAP slippage — the extra cost you pay to fill the entire trade at the current best ask on the live Polymarket orderbook. The friction badge on the right is independent of the latency gauge above — it only reports depth cost. A green badge with red-tier latency still means your order lands late; the price may have moved before your copy arrives.

VWAP extra cost on Polymarket’s currently-active 15-minute prediction markets. Same metric as the 5-min column, but the timer is less frantic — liquidity usually settles a bit as the resolution window approaches. Copy-Trade Friction Rating? Copy-Trade Friction Rating.
